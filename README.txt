Tax Computation & Filing Assistant for Freelancers & Small Businesses (PH)
Overview

This app is a tax filing and computation assistant designed for Philippine non-VAT freelancers and small business owners.

It helps users:

- Compute how much tax is due

- Prepare accurate figures for BIR filing

- Keep clean records that can be exported if required

- This app is not a bookkeeping or accounting system.

Who This App Is For:

- Freelancers and solo professionals

- Non-VAT taxpayers

Individuals filing:

- 1701Q / 1701A

- 2551Q

- Users who want clarity and correctness, not accounting complexity

What This App Does:

- Records income and expenses in a simple, tax-focused format

- Automatically computes:

- Income tax (8% or graduated, as applicable)

- Percentage tax

- Withholding tax offsets (CWT/WT)

- Produces quarterly and annual summaries

- Aligns totals with BIR tax return requirements

- Allows backup of records for compliance or reference

What This App Is NOT

❌ A bookkeeping system

❌ A journal or ledger replacement

❌ An accounting software

❌ A substitute for a CPA in complex cases

- Disclaimer -

This app assists with tax computation and record organization.
It does not replace professional advice for complex tax situations.

📄 FULL APP DESCRIPTION

This application is a non-VAT income and expense tracking and tax computation assistant designed for freelancers, consultants, small service businesses, and bookkeepers who currently rely on manual Excel spreadsheets for tax filing.

The app allows users to record income and expenses in a structured way and automatically generate tax summaries needed for quarterly and annual non-VAT income tax filing (such as totals and computed tax figures). By automating calculations and summaries, it helps reduce common manual errors, such as incorrect formulas, miscalculations, and inconsistent totals that often happen with spreadsheets.

The primary purpose of the app is to support the tax filing process, not to replace professional judgment or official filing systems. All computations are based on the data entered by the user, and final accuracy still depends on correct input and proper review. The tool is meant to act as a practical assistant—making record-keeping simpler, clearer, and more organized—so users can file with more confidence.

This app is especially useful for:

Non-VAT freelancers and self-employed individuals who file their own taxes

Bookkeepers and accountants handling multiple non-VAT clients

Small service businesses that want a simple and consistent way to track income and expenses

Overall, the goal of the app is to reduce repetitive manual work, minimize calculation-related mistakes, and make non-VAT tax preparation less stressful, while still allowing users or professionals to review and finalize filings properly.
